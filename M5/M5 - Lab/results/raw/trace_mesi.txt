Snooping-bus teaching model, 1 cache line, 2 cores (THEORETICAL, not hardware output)

=== Trace 1: read-only sharing (both start in I)  [MESI] ===
Step  Event                  Bus traffic                              C0   C1  
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1     C0 reads x             BusRd; no sharer answers                 E    I   
2     C1 reads x             BusRd; sharer signals shared             S    S   
3     Both read x again      None (hit)                               S    S   

=== Trace 2: C0 writes after both read (both start in S)  [MESI] ===
Step  Event                  Bus traffic                              C0   C1  
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1     C0 executes x = 1      BusUpgr; others invalidate               M    I   
2     C1 later reads x       BusRd; C0 flushes + writes back          S    S   
3     C1 reads x again       None (hit)                               S    S   

=== Trace 3: competing writers (ownership ping-pong)  [MESI] ===
Step  Event                  Bus traffic                              C0   C1  
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1     C0 writes x            BusRdX                                   M    I   
2     C1 writes x            BusRdX; C0 flushes + invalidates         I    M   
3     C0 writes x again      BusRdX; C1 flushes + invalidates         M    I   
4     C1 writes x again      BusRdX; C0 flushes + invalidates         I    M   

Key difference: Trace 2 step 2 -> MESI gives C0=S, MOESI gives C0=O.
